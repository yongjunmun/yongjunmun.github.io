Personal · Low-latency systems

Limit Order Book & Matching Engine

A limit order book, price-time-priority matching engine, and NASDAQ ITCH 5.0 decoder written from scratch in header-only C++23 with no dependencies. A direct-indexed price array with a two-level bitset summary cancels in constant time at any queue depth: 142 ns with 100,000 orders resting on one price level, against 198 ns for a random cancel. Benchmarked in the same process against a textbook std::map book, whose worst-case add is 148 times slower.

  • C++23
